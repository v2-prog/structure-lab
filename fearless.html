Fearless Folder

Room 11. Pape’s question is the brief: if you love them, would you leave them a mess? The folder is the individual layer of Succession 321, built for a NSW resident. Tick what exists. Ticks stay in this browser only.

His published list is short: will, powers of attorney, super binding nomination, accounts and investments, logins, funeral instructions, and a final letter. Originals in a bolted safe. Scans in a dedicated estate email with app-based two-factor authentication, not SMS. Source: The controlling husband — Fearless Folder, August 2025.

NSW papers that belong in it

DocumentWhat it doesNSW note
Will Directs estate assets after death. Pape’s public line is a lawyer this month, not a will kit. Review after marriage, separation, a child, or a major asset change.
Enduring power of attorney Financial and legal decisions if you lose capacity. Must be made while you have capacity. See NSW Local Court.
Appointment of enduring guardian Health and lifestyle decisions. Takes effect only on loss of capacity. Witness must be eligible — solicitor, barrister, Local Court registrar, or a NSW Trustee and Guardian officer. Legal Aid NSW.
Advance care directive Wishes for future medical treatment. NSW has no statutory ACD scheme. A common-law directive can bind if it was voluntary, made with capacity, and clear enough for the decision. Advance Care Planning Australia, NSW Health.
Super death-benefit nomination Directs super and attached insurance. Super generally does not pass under the will. Binding or non-binding depends on the fund. If it is paid to the estate, the will then governs. ATO death benefits.

Also in the folder, not in the will

  • Funeral wishes and organ donation. The will is often read too late.
  • A “when I’m gone” register: accounts, policies, advisers, titles, where the originals are.
  • Password-manager emergency access. Crypto, subscriptions, email, MyGov. Do not tape a master password to the will.
  • Life, TPD and income-protection schedules, checked against debts and dependants. His columns treat this as the protector’s job, separate from step 7 (the mortgage).
  • SMSF: trustee and director succession under the deed and the corporate-trustee constitution. Control of the fund is the succession issue.
  • A short letter. He asks for one. It is not a legal instrument.

Folder checklist

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust. The folder records control. It does not move title.
