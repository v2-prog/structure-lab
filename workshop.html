Scenario workshop

Guided sketches. Still not calculations. Questions a solicitor and tax agent will ask, plus CGT and duty watch-fors.

Sell land now vs hold

Holding defers a disposal. Selling usually crystallises a CGT event. Neither path is “better” here.

  • Whose name is on title, and is that the economic owner?
  • Pre-20 September 1985 acquisition?
  • Main-residence periods, absence, change of use?
  • Cost base: improvements, subdivisions, related-party dealings?
  • CGT event A1 on a sale is the common flag. Market-value substitution if not arm’s length.
  • Selling from a trust or company is not the same event as selling in personal names.

Gift during life vs testamentary gift

  • Recipient: family, charity, CLT, or ancillary fund?
  • Does the donor still need the asset for housing or income?
  • Lifetime gift of a CGT asset: market-value substitution is the usual flag.
  • Duty on a gift of dutiable property is State-specific. Do not assume a love-and-affection exemption.
  • A testamentary gift to a charity is not the same as a living DGR deduction.

Move land into a CLT or charity

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.

  • Is the receiving body incorporated, with an asset lock and responsible persons?
  • Gift, sale at undervalue, or long ground lease?
  • CGT on the way in is common. Rollovers are narrow. Do not invent one.
  • Duty concessions, if any, are not uniform across Australia.

Pay adult children from a trust vs wages from a company

  • Identifiable work, or only family support?
  • Employer: company, trustee, or no one?
  • Div 6AA — higher tax flags on some unexcepted trust income of minors.
  • s100A — reimbursement-agreement flags where someone else enjoys the benefit.
  • Wages create PAYG, super and workers compensation duties.

Debt recycle / offset vs structure change

  • Existing loan: home, investment, or mixed?
  • Would a redraw, split or offset avoid moving title?
  • Interest deductibility follows use of the borrowed money, not the product label.
  • Moving land to “make the debt work” often triggers CGT and duty. This lab will not estimate that gap.
