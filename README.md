# Structure Lab AU

Acer Lab’s Australia-only explainer for family, land and purpose structures.

**Canonical site:** https://acerlab.link

General information only. Not tax, legal or financial advice. Confirm with a registered tax agent and solicitor. Rules include 2026–28 announced reforms that may change.

A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.

`earthacer.in` stays parked at Hostinger. Do not point it at this project.

## What this is

A static eight-room notebook:

1. Home
2. Household profile (answers stored in `localStorage` only)
3. Structure explorer — nineteen Australian structures
4. Scenario workshop
5. CLT / charity / co-op studio
6. Compare
7. Action checklist
8. Glossary & timeline

No accounts. No lead-capture. No invented duty tables or dollar savings.
