Action checklist

Informational next steps. Not a paid service.

Talk to a registered tax agent about the current CGT, trust and fund position — including 2026–28 announced reforms.
See a solicitor about the trust deed, will, enduring documents and any binding death benefit nomination.
Confirm title, easements and the State or Territory duty position with the titles / revenue office for that jurisdiction.
If a purpose entity is in view: constitution or deed, asset lock wording, responsible persons, and whether ACNC / DGR is even on the path.
If an SMSF is in play: current deed, trustee form, investment strategy and auditor — before any related-party land idea.
Do not move land, grant a covenant, or accept a “simple” minute until those conversations have happened.
If First Nations land or a neighbouring Aboriginal land body is involved, take advice in that statute. This lab is not that advice.

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.
