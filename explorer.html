Structure explorer

Nineteen Australian structures and overlays. Flags only. No dollar rates.

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.

01 · DT
Discretionary / family trustasset lock: no

What it is
A trust deed lets a trustee choose how to distribute income and capital among a class of beneficiaries.
Who it is for
Households holding investments or a business for a family group.
Who controls it
Trustee (often a company). Appointor / principal usually holds the real control.
Tax character
Flow-through to beneficiaries, subject to trust law and anti-avoidance flags.
Duty / CGT
Land into a trust is often a duty event. CGT on settlement unless a rollover applies — ask, do not assume.
Wrong tool when
The job is an irreversible community purpose, or the deed and appointor are neglected.

02 · UT
Unit trustasset lock: no

What it is
Beneficiaries hold units. Income and capital usually follow unit holding.
Who it is for
Joint investors who want defined shares.
Who controls it
Trustee. Unitholders vote per the deed.
Tax character
Often flow-through. Unit sales can be CGT events.
Duty / CGT
Flag landholder duty on unit issues or transfers that hold land.
Wrong tool when
The family wanted open discretion, or a purpose lock is required.

03 · HT
Hybrid trustasset lock: no

What it is
A deed mixing discretionary classes with some unit or fixed rights.
Who it is for
Where both family discretion and a defined investor slice are wanted.
Who controls it
Deed-specific. Read appointor, unit and default clauses first.
Tax character
Follows the deed and how it is administered. High review risk if used casually.
Duty / CGT
Same land-in and CGT-on-entry flags as other trusts.
Wrong tool when
Used as a DIY template. Hybrid deeds are easy to misunderstand.

04 · BT
Bare trustasset lock: no

What it is
Trustee holds legal title; beneficiary is absolutely entitled.
Who it is for
Nominee arrangements and some SMSF property titles.
Who controls it
Beneficiary directs. Trustee has almost no discretion.
Tax character
Usually looked through to the beneficiary. Confirm with the agent.
Duty / CGT
Duty may still see a change of beneficial ownership.
Wrong tool when
Anyone needs discretion or an asset lock.

05 · TT
Testamentary trustasset lock: no

What it is
A trust created by a will that starts on death.
Who it is for
Families planning how children or a surviving partner take wealth after death.
Who controls it
Will-appointed trustee.
Tax character
Excepted-trust income flags for some minor beneficiaries may apply — confirm current law.
Duty / CGT
Death pathways differ from a lifetime gift. Do not treat them as the same.
Wrong tool when
The person wants a purpose gift to take effect now.

06 · CO
Company (Pty Ltd)asset lock: no

What it is
A separate legal person. Shareholders own shares; directors run the company.
Who it is for
Trading businesses and some family investment companies.
Who controls it
Directors day to day. Shareholders for major decisions.
Tax character
Company tax, then franked dividends. Not flow-through.
Duty / CGT
Landholder duty on share dealings that hold land. Share sale ≠ land sale.
Wrong tool when
The household needed flow-through or a community asset lock.

07 · PA
Partnershipasset lock: no

What it is
Two or more people in business together under State partnership law.
Who it is for
Simple joint businesses.
Who controls it
Partners, subject to the agreement.
Tax character
Usually flow-through of partnership income.
Duty / CGT
Change of partners can be a duty or CGT event where land is a partnership asset.
Wrong tool when
Limited liability or a community asset lock is required.

08 · ST
Sole traderasset lock: no

What it is
An individual trading in their own name. An ABN does not create a new person.
Who it is for
Simple personal enterprises.
Who controls it
The individual.
Tax character
Personal tax rates on the net income.
Duty / CGT
The person and the land are not separated.
Wrong tool when
Several people, employees, or a purpose lock enter the picture.

09 · SF
SMSFasset lock: conditional

What it is
A self-managed superannuation fund. Sole-purpose test and SIS Act rules apply.
Who it is for
Members who accept trustee duties for retirement savings.
Who controls it
Trustee (individuals or corporate). Members are usually the trustees.
Tax character
Fund rates while complying; different treatment in pension phase. Confirm current law.
Duty / CGT
Related-party property and in-house asset rules. Duty on land into the fund is State-specific.
Wrong tool when
Holiday house, community settlement, or as a family discretionary bucket.

10 · FIC
Family investment companyasset lock: no

What it is
A Pty Ltd used mainly to hold investments for a family group. Pattern, not a separate Act.
Who it is for
Families comfortable with company tax and franking.
Who controls it
Directors and shareholders. Shareholder agreements do the real work.
Tax character
Company tax, then dividends. Not a trust.
Duty / CGT
Same flags as other companies that hold land.
Wrong tool when
The family needed trust flow-through or a locked community purpose.

11 · CLT
Community land trustasset lock: yes

What it is
Holds land for community affordability or stewardship, usually with an asset lock and ground leases.
Who it is for
Groups that want land kept out of ordinary family wealth extraction.
Who controls it
Board under a constitution. Residents typically hold a lease, not the freehold.
Tax character
Depends on charity / NFP status. Do not assume a concession.
Duty / CGT
Moving private land in is often a CGT event and a duty event.
Wrong tool when
Used as a stand-in for a family discretionary trust.

12 · CT
Charitable trustasset lock: yes

What it is
A trust for charitable purposes recognised by Australian charity law.
Who it is for
Donors who want a purpose, not a family class of beneficiaries.
Who controls it
Trustee bound by the purpose. ACNC registration is a separate question.
Tax character
Charity concessions are not automatic. Deductibility of gifts is another question.
Duty / CGT
Settling CGT assets can trigger CGT. Duty exemptions, if any, are State-specific.
Wrong tool when
Family wealth planning. The purpose is not a family.

13 · CLG
Company limited by guaranteeasset lock: often

What it is
A company without share capital. Members guarantee a nominal amount.
Who it is for
Associations that need a company structure and an asset lock in the constitution.
Who controls it
Board. Members do not own equity in the ordinary sense.
Tax character
Follows charity / NFP status if obtained. No family dividend story.
Duty / CGT
Land in still needs a duty and CGT check.
Wrong tool when
Paying family members as owners.

14 · IA
Incorporated associationasset lock: often

What it is
A State or Territory association with legal personality under local associations law.
Who it is for
Smaller community groups, including some land-care and housing groups.
Who controls it
Committee. Members under the rules.
Tax character
NFP character is common; charity status is separate.
Duty / CGT
Capacity to hold land depends on that State or Territory statute.
Wrong tool when
Multi-State operations that needed a company, or family investment.

15 · CP
Co-operativeasset lock: often

What it is
A member-owned body under co-operatives law. Active membership and limited returns are typical.
Who it is for
Groups sharing use of land or enterprise who want member control.
Who controls it
Board elected by members.
Tax character
Varies. Not family-trust discretion.
Duty / CGT
Co-op shares tied to land can raise duty flags.
Wrong tool when
The real goal was a locked charity or a family investment company.

16 · FN
First Nations land-holding patternsasset lock: varies

What it is
High-level flags only: Aboriginal Land Trusts, Prescribed Bodies Corporate, Local Aboriginal Land Councils.
Who it is for
Communities and partners who need to know these are distinct from a private family trust or CLT.
Who controls it
Statute and community governance — not a private appointor.
Tax character
Specific statutory and native title settings. This lab is not Native Title advice.
Duty / CGT
Unique consent, duty and CGT pathways. Do not analogise from a private trust.
Wrong tool when
Treated as a private structuring option, or assumed to be the same as a private CLT.

17 · LI
Life interestasset lock: no

What it is
A right to use property for life, with remainder to someone else. Often created by will.
Who it is for
Keeping a person in a house while preserving remainder for children or a purpose.
Who controls it
Life tenant vs remainder beneficiary.
Tax character
CGT and trust-income flags depend on how the interest is created.
Duty / CGT
Creating or ending a life interest in land can be a CGT event and, in some cases, a duty event.
Wrong tool when
A simple outright gift was wanted, or a community lock on the freehold.

18 · CV
Covenant / conservation agreementasset lock: overlay

What it is
A land overlay, not a holding vehicle. Restricts use while title stays where it is.
Who it is for
Owners who want a use restriction without moving land into a charity.
Who controls it
Registered interest binds later owners.
Tax character
Possible CGT pathways on granting some conservation covenants — flags only, no rate.
Duty / CGT
Registration on title is the point. CGT and valuation matter more than duty in many cases.
Wrong tool when
The group needed a new owner. A covenant does not create a CLT.

19 · AF
Ancillary fund (PuAF / PAF)asset lock: yes

What it is
A DGR-category fund that gives to other deductible recipients. Public and private forms exist.
Who it is for
Households or groups that want a structured giving vehicle.
Who controls it
Trustee under the ancillary fund guidelines.
Tax character
Gift deductibility is the usual reason people look here. It is a pathway, not a promise.
Duty / CGT
Gifting CGT assets into the fund is often a CGT event. Do not assume a rollover.
Wrong tool when
Holding the family farm for the family, or as a CLT substitute.

First Nations holding forms are listed so they are not confused with a private family trust. This page is not Native Title advice.
