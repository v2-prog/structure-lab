Date night and buckets

Room 10. Scott Pape’s Barefoot steps are the operating rhythm under Succession 321. Do the conversation before the documents. This page uses his published step names. It is not the book, and it is not advice.

▣General information only. A date night does not replace a will, a binding nomination, or a solicitor. Confirm with a registered tax agent and a wills-and-estates lawyer.

The nine steps, in his order

Pape’s published sequence is deliberately slow. Succession sits at the end, but the earlier steps are what make a plan fundable. Source: Barefoot Steps.

  1. Date night. One monthly sitting. Accounts, decisions, and the hard conversation — not a lecture in the kitchen.
  2. Buckets. The serviette: Blow, Mojo, Grow. Money on autopilot.
  3. Domino the debts. Smallest balance first, then the next. Consumer debt is a succession problem if it dies with you.
  4. Buy the home. The main residence is often the largest estate asset and the one most exposed to probate delay.
  5. Super to 15 per cent. His published target. Super does not follow the will unless it is paid to the estate.
  6. Mojo to three months. Living expenses, in a separate account, so a funeral week is not a credit-card week.
  7. Banker off your back. Mortgage, attacked on purpose. His step 7 is the loan, not the insurance policy.
  8. Retirement number. Donald Bradman strategy in the book — a number, not a vibe.
  9. Leave a legacy. How you want to be remembered. That is the door into the Fearless Folder and Succession 321.

Date night is the succession meeting

Treat the monthly sitting as the family meeting the succession guide asks for. Same table, same notebook, one decision per night. Pape’s instruction is to get dressed up and actually do the step, not discuss it. For a household with a trust or a business, the agenda rotates:

  • This month: buckets and Mojo balance.
  • Next: will, enduring documents, and whether the super nomination is still binding.
  • Then: who is the backup if the principal is in hospital tonight.
  • Once a year: the one-page map in room 12.

If you are single, the sitting still happens. Coffee, notebook, same questions. A successor does not require a spouse.

Three buckets, and what each must hand over

The published serviette is three buckets. Income lands, then it is split. Secondary summaries of the book put the Blow split at about 60 per cent daily expenses, 10 per cent Splurge, 10 per cent Smile and 20 per cent Fire Extinguisher. Those ratios are a cashflow habit, not a legal rule — change them if the household cannot eat. What matters for succession is that each bucket has a named backup.

BucketJob, in his wordsSuccession question
BlowDaily expenses, a splurge, and cash to fight a fire.Who can pay the rent and the school fees in week one?
MojoSafety money. His target is three months of living expenses.Is it in an account the executor or attorney can actually reach?
GrowLong-term wealth: super, home equity, investments, the business.Does control move by will, by deed, by nomination, or not at all?

Mojo is the incapacity buffer. Insurance, if it pays, pays later. Three months is his published character test — see step 6 on the steps page. It is not a substitute for life, TPD or income-protection cover.

What this room does not do

It does not calculate a retirement number, quote a premium, or tell you to salary-sacrifice. Super to 15 per cent is his step, not a cap ruling. Contribution caps, Division 293 and the transfer balance cap sit with a registered tax agent. The ATO contribution caps are the check.
