CLT / charity / co-op studio

Purpose land. Nothing here is an application for DGR or ACNC registration.

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.

Asset lock vs family wealth

An asset lock stops members extracting the land as share capital. Family wealth tools keep control and a path to benefit a class of people. If both jobs exist, they are two instruments, not one wrapper.

Ground lease vs freehold inside a CLT

Many CLTs keep freehold locked and grant long ground leases. The household may own the dwelling and lease the dirt. Ask what happens on assignment, death or default; whether the lease is dutiable; who can refuse a sale.

DGR pathway (conceptual)

Deductible Gift Recipient status is an ATO endorsement. Pathway: a purpose that fits a category; the right form; endorsement. This lab does not promise DGR.

Co-op vs company limited by guarantee vs charitable trust

  • Co-op — member use and limited returns.
  • Company limited by guarantee — no shares; lock belongs in the constitution.
  • Charitable trust — purpose in a deed; trustee duties are the whole story.

Why a purpose wrapper does not replace a family discretionary trust

If land is locked to the purpose, it is no longer a family investment. If it remains extractable by the family, it is not locked.

First Nations land-trust ideas

Aboriginal Land Trusts, Prescribed Bodies Corporate and Local Aboriginal Land Councils are statutory and community forms. Adjacent models, not templates for a private eco-settlement, and not Native Title advice.
