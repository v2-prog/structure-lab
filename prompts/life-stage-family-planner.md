# Life-Stage Family Planner — App Prompt

**Status:** Draft for later build-out
**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Source:** Voice conversation with Grok (Eve)

---

## The Prompt

Build an Australian life-stage planning app for families with little or no financial literacy. The hook is a family tree: grandparents, parents, children, grandchildren. Each person gets variables — marital status, spouse, deceased, ages. Life events like coming of age, marriage, bereavement, or a child starting school act as triggers. When one fires, the app surfaces the specific, invisible things worth considering at that exact moment — Centrelink payments, tax thresholds, super rules, school transitions, estate steps — with plain-language explanations and links to support resources and official material. Everything is general information, not personal advice, flagged clearly. The interface uses progressive disclosure: only the next relevant step appears, never the whole map. Tone is warm, never condescending. Australia only.

---

## Design Principles Behind It

1. **People first, not features.** Who is it for? A teacher planning retirement, a family running a co-op, a charity chasing DGR status?
2. **The journey each person walks.** What decisions hit at what age, what rules change, what deadlines matter.
3. **Make the invisible visible.** Tax caps, contribution limits, grant windows, compliance steps — so someone isn't scrambling when a form is due in six weeks.
4. **Trust.** If it touches money or legal structures, be clear about what's general information and what needs a professional.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Only see the next step, never the whole staircase.
6. **Absolute specificity.** "Your daughter turns eighteen next year, here's when youth allowance and tax-free thresholds kick in" beats reading about superannuation in general.
7. **The gap isn't intelligence — it's access.** Nobody teaches you the questions to ask until you've already been caught out.
