### .com.au / .au pricing (Australia, Oct 2026)
- Wholesale .au fee rose from $8.64 to $9.50 ex GST on 1 Oct 2026 (~10% increase).
- Cheapest .com.au: Porkbun ~$7.48 first year (promo), ~$8.48 renewal. Above.com $7.60, Hostinger $7.64.
- Typical .com.au: $18–25/yr. VentraIP ~$20–22. Cloudflare Registrar at-cost ~$10 for .com (not available for .com.au).
- .au direct (second-level) also available since 2022 — same eligibility as .com.au, shorter.
- .com.au requires Australian business registration (ABN, ACN, or registered trademark) and must match or relate to the registered business name.
- Namecheap: .com ~$18–22, .com.au ~$25, free WHOIS privacy on .com.

### Not-for-profit angle
- org.au available for not-for-profits, ~$12–15/yr at wholesale end (Micron21 $14.91, Quantum Core $12.50).
- Domain must match or relate to the registered not-for-profit name.
- Pairs with DGR exploration: tax-deductible donations strengthen funding story vs grants alone.
- ACNC registration needed before org.au.
