Glossary & timeline

Short definitions. Confirm current law. No dates here are legal deadlines.

CGT event
The moment the tax law treats a CGT asset as disposed of or otherwise brought to account. Sale is only one example.
Cost base
The figure the law starts from when working out a capital gain or loss.
Market value substitution
Can replace the actual price with market value, often on gifts or related-party deals.
s100A
Anti-avoidance provision aimed at some trust reimbursement agreements. Flag, not a verdict.
Div 6AA
Can apply a higher rate to some unexcepted trust income of minors.
DGR
Deductible Gift Recipient. An endorsement, not a vibe.
Asset lock
A rule that stops owners extracting the asset as private wealth.
Ground lease
A long lease of the land itself, often used by a CLT while a household owns the dwelling.
Life interest
A right to use property for life, with a remainder to someone else.
Testamentary trust
A trust that starts under a will, on death.
Duty
State and Territory tax on certain dutiable transactions. Tables are not published here.
Main residence
CGT settings for a home are full of conditions. Flag only.
2026–28 reforms
Announced changes may alter settings. Confirm current law with the agent.

Typical order of work

  1. Advice — tax agent and solicitor, with the title search and existing deeds on the table.
  2. Documents — will, BDBN, deed amendments, constitution, loan splits. Still no movement of land.
  3. Stamping / lodgement — duty, titles, ASIC / ACNC / revenue forms as required.
  4. Land movement — transfer, lease, covenant or gift, only after the above.

▣A charity or community land trust with an asset lock cannot be a substitute for a family discretionary trust.
